# Team Health Holdings

TeamHealth Holdings is one of the largest physician practice organizations in the United States, providing outsourced healthcare professional staffing and administrative services to hospitals and other healthcare providers. Through more than 15,000 affiliated healthcare professionals and advanced practice clinicians, TeamHealth offers emergency medicine, hospital medicine, critical care, anesthesiology, orthopedic surgery, general surgery, obstetrics, ambulatory care, post-acute care, and medical call center solutions to approximately 3,000 acute and post-acute facilities and physician groups nationwide.

**Website:** [https://www.teamhealth.com](https://www.teamhealth.com)  
**Careers:** [https://www.teamhealthcareers.com](https://www.teamhealthcareers.com)

## APIs

TeamHealth Holdings does not maintain a public-facing developer API. This repository documents the organization's service domain and vocabulary.

## Service Lines

- **Emergency Medicine** - Physician staffing for hospital emergency departments
- **Hospital Medicine** - Hospitalist physician services for inpatient care
- **Critical Care** - Intensivist staffing for ICUs
- **Anesthesiology** - Anesthesiology staffing and practice management
- **Ambulatory Care** - Outpatient care physician services
- **Post-Acute Care** - Physician services for rehabilitation and SNF facilities
- **Medical Call Center** - 24/7 nurse triage and care coordination services
